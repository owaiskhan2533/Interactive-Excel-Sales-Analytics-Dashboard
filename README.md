# Dynamic Hardware Sales & Representative Performance Dashboard

## Executive Summary
This **Interactive MS Excel Dashboard** provides a multi-tab analytics platform to monitor electronic hardware sales performance, regional revenue distribution, and sales representative productivity[cite: 13, 14, 15]. Designed with an intuitive side-navigation menu, it enables managers to drill down into product-level insights and identify top and bottom-performing sales reps[cite: 13, 14, 15].

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Sales Revenue:** ₹17,82,570[cite: 13]
* **Total Order Volume (Sales):** 366 Units[cite: 13]
* **Top Revenue Region:** East Region (₹7,36,080)[cite: 13]
* **Top Selling Product:** Printer (52.42% share)[cite: 13]
* **Top Sales Executive:** Siddhu (₹5,51,960 total sales revenue)[cite: 15]

---

## Tools & Features Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Custom shape UI components, macro/hyperlink dynamic tab navigation (`Dashboard`, `Product`, `Salesman`, `About`).
* **Interactive Slicers & Timeline:** Date Timeline Slicer (2021–2022), Regional Slicers (`East`, `West`, `North`, `South`), and Product Item Slicers (`Keyboard`, `Monitor`, `Mouse`, `Printer`, `Scanner`, `Speaker`)[cite: 13, 14, 15, 16].
* **Advanced Charting:** KPI Cards, Monthly Sales Area/Line Trends, Bar Charts, Horizontal Ranking Charts[cite: 13, 14, 15].

---

## Tab Breakdown & Dashboard Features

### 1. Analytics Overview (Dashboard Tab)
* **Revenue & Volume KPIs:** Tracks Total Amount (₹17.82 Lakhs) and Total Sales Units (366)[cite: 13].
* **Sales by Product:** Share percentage across items — Printer (52.42%), Scanner (19.76%), Monitor (19.02%), Mouse (3.71%), Speaker (3.67%), and Keyboard (1.42%)[cite: 13].
* **Sales by Region:** Revenue breakdown across **East (₹7,36,080)**, **West (₹5,07,330)**, **South (₹3,35,480)**, and **North (₹2,03,680)**[cite: 13].
* **Monthly Sales Trend:** Trajectory of monthly sales throughout the year[cite: 13].

### 2. Product Analysis (Product Tab)
* **Product Volume Breakdown:** Unit sales across hardware items[cite: 14].
* **Top 3 Selling Products:** **Printer (445 units)**, **Mouse (319 units)**, and **Monitor (251 units)**[cite: 14].
* **Top 3 Less Selling Products:** **Keyboard (132 units)**, **Speaker (218 units)**, and **Scanner (242 units)**[cite: 14].

### 3. Salesperson Performance (Salesman Tab)
* **Sales by Executive:** Individual revenue contributions — **Siddhu (₹5,51,960)**, **Rohit Das (₹3,94,410)**, **Ajit Kumar (₹3,86,030)**, **Amit (₹1,84,690)**, **Ramesh (₹1,39,880)**, and **Chandu (₹1,25,600)**[cite: 15].
* **Top Performing Salesmen:** Siddhu (419 units), Ajit Kumar (394 units), and Rohit Das (360 units)[cite: 15].
* **Underperforming Salesmen:** Ramesh (144 units), Chandu (144 units), and Amit (146 units)[cite: 15].

## Key Business Insights
1. **Product Revenue Concentration:** **Printers** generate more than half of the total revenue (52.42%), making them the primary core product[cite: 13].
2. **Geographic Strengths:** The **East** and **West** regions combined generate over 69% of overall revenue (₹12.43 Lakhs)[cite: 13].
3. **Salesforce Productivity Gap:** High performers like **Siddhu** generate nearly 4.4x the revenue of bottom performers like **Chandu**, indicating a need for targeted sales coaching or territory reassignment[cite: 15].
